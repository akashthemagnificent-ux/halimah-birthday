    the night you came into this world

    This is the Rosette Nebula, a rose-shaped cloud of hydrogen about five thousand light-years away. It is a nursery: brand-new stars are being born inside it, right now.

    On the night you arrived, this is what the deep sky was quietly holding: a place where stars get made. It felt right to keep it beside your birthday.

    photographed by NASA

    26 August 2026

    On the night we chose each other, the sky was already holding a ring. We did not plan that. It is simply what was there.
